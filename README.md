## 🧭 My Journey

It started with a degree in **Informatics Engineering** from UPN "Veteran" Yogyakarta (S.T., Cum Laude, 2019) — where I fell in love with how data could tell a story.

Fresh out of university, I joined **PT Bank Mandiri** through PT Anugrah Karya Abadi, where I got my hands dirty building ETL pipelines with Talend and SQL Server. One of my proudest early wins: **automating financial reports that cut manual effort by 80%**. That moment taught me that good data engineering isn't just technical — it creates real business impact.

That momentum carried me to **PT Bank BTPN Syariah** (via PT Avows Technologies), where I've spent nearly 6 years architecting enterprise-scale data solutions. From migrating on-premise data warehouses to AWS (including more than **2.2 billion rows** of historical data), moving pipeline scheduling from Control-M to AWS Step Functions, and modernizing the data lake from Apache Hudi to Apache Iceberg, to building Tableau dashboards that help stakeholders make smarter decisions — I've learned that data pipelines are only as good as the insights they unlock.

Today, I specialize in **end-to-end data engineering** — from raw ingestion to cloud warehousing to BI — with a focus on the banking and financial sector.
